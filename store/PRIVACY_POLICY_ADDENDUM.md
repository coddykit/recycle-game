## Borderline Empire (com.coddykit.borderlineempire)

Borderline Empire is a single-player logistics game published by Mehmet Canker / CoddyKit. No game account is required. Company name, game progress and settings remain on the device and are not uploaded as saved-game or profile data. Reset Progress deletes local empire progress; purchase ownership and sponsor cooldown are maintained separately. Uninstalling removes local game progress.

The app uses Firebase Analytics to measure sessions, screen views and gameplay milestones using app instance identifiers. Company names and saved games are not sent to Analytics. Advertising consent signals for Analytics remain denied; automated QA mode excludes collection.

Version 1.2.0 (19 October 2026) adds an in-game shop with more optional one-time products and cash packs, cross-promotion of the developer's other games, an interstitial ad between play sessions and an in-game feedback form. Players who own Remove Ads, Kingpin Edition, the Ultimate Bundle or the Empire Pass (and anyone who had bought something before this rule took effect) never see interstitial ads; cash packs and looks alone do not remove them.

Optional rewarded ads and the interstitial ads are provided by Google AdMob; before AdMob, the game may show a cross-promotion for another CoddyKit game. To choose it the game downloads the ad schedule from CoddyKit's server (Supabase) and, when an ad is shown or tapped, sends CoddyKit a random installation identifier, the ad's app id and the device model; no account or advertising ID is used for this. AdMob may process device identifiers, network information, ad interactions and diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Google's consent flow is used where required; advertising privacy choices are available in the Shop. Core gameplay does not require an advertisement.

The in-app feedback form (Settings → Send feedback) sends the e-mail address and message the player types, the chosen category, the app version, language, platform, OS version and device model to CoddyKit's feedback service so the team can reply; nothing is sent unless the player presses Send.

The one-time purchases (Empire Pass, Kingpin Edition, upgrades, looks and bundles) and the repeatable cash packs are processed through the platform store and validated by RevenueCat using purchase/transaction records and an anonymous app user identifier. The developer does not receive payment card details. Restore Purchases retrieves previously purchased one-time products using the same store account (cash packs are used up and not restored). There is no subscription.

For privacy questions and data requests, contact coddy@coddykit.com. Requests concerning transaction records may also need to be addressed to the platform store. Game progress has no server-side cloud copy.
