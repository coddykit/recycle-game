# Rewarded ads and IAP
The owner selected rewarded advertising plus IAP. Core play remains free. Sponsor
rewards require a native earned-reward callback and have a ten-minute cooldown.
A one-time Empire Pass gives +25% delivery income and instant sponsor claims with
the same cooldown. No forced ads, subscriptions or trial claims. Show store-fetched
localized prices only; support restore, cancellation, pending activation, duplicate
callbacks and unavailable SDKs. Purchased rights survive progress reset/prestige.
