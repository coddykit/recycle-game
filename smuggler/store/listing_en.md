## Full description

Every empire starts with one rusty hatchback.

Meet Dot, a retired dispatcher who knows every back road in eight made-up countries. Name your
company, pick its colour and start taking jobs nobody else will touch. Fast routes pay more.
Safe routes keep the heat down. You make the call.

**RUN THE ROUTES**
• Pick contracts, choose a vehicle and a route (Fast, Normal or Safe), then watch the delivery
  roll across a hand-crafted low-poly world.
• Handle surprises on the road: rival crews, inspections, breakdowns, road closures and nervous passengers.
• Keep each region's Heat in check, or the next crossing gets a lot harder.

**GROW THE BUSINESS**
• Upgrade 10 vehicles: engine, capacity, navigation, suspension and more.
• Hire drivers with their own rarity and skills.
• Expand your HQ from a dusty garage into an Underground HQ.
• Unlock 8 fictional regions, each with its own landscape and border crossing.

**AUTOMATE THE EMPIRE**
• Auto Accept, Auto Assign, Auto Dispatch, then Regional Managers run the show while you plan.
• Earn Influence and spend it on permanent Connections.
• Reach Empire status, prestige and come back stronger with Legacy Stars.

**MADE FOR SHORT BREAKS**
• Satisfying 30-second sessions, with earnings while you're away.
• One-thumb portrait controls.
• Fully voiced mentor and announcer, in English and Turkish.
• Play without an account. Core gameplay works offline.
• Optional sponsor rewards and a one-time Empire Pass; no forced ads.

All countries, goods and organisations in the game are fictional. Risk is shown only as
numbers.
