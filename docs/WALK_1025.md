# Smuggler character walking — 2026-10-25 (W68)

Root cause: `character_actor.gd` advanced the walk at a fixed 9 rad/m and swung the rigid legs on a sine (±0.7 rad):
a leg of 0.18 × scale sweeps 2·L·sin(0.7) per half cycle, ~14 % less than the body moves, so every stance foot slid
forward; the swing foot never lifted.

Fix (`scripts/world/character_actor.gd`, script only — no geometry, draw or material change):
- Cycle by metres walked: stride = 4 · L · scale · sin(0.7) (0.60 m at scale 1.3, ~2 cycles/s at 1.2 m/s).
- Stance half: leg angle = asin(reach · (1 − 4p)) so the foot moves back exactly as fast as the body moves forward;
  the body sinks L(1 − cos) so the sole stays on the floor. Swing half: leg scales to 0.86 (shoe lifts), eases forward.
- Arms swing opposite to the leg on their side (carry pose unchanged); standing = straight legs, scale 1.

Measured (tools/walk_probe.gd, 3 real actors, slower foot travel / body travel; 0 = planted): 0.451 → 0.053.
Low cost: identical node/draw count; a few float ops per walking actor per frame.
Evidence: /Volumes/BuildSSD/game-builds/w68/walking-1025/smuggler/ (walk-before-after-5s.mp4 top = before, strip.png).
